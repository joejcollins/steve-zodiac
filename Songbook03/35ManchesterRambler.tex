\begin{SBSong}{Manchester Rambler}
    {$Id: 35ManchesterRambler.tex 810 2013-04-21 21:00:48Z ludwigvanboll@gmail.com $}
    {Rambler Channel, separates Tsing Yi Island and the mainland New Territories in Hong Kong}
    {Ewan MacColl}
  \SBChord{C}
  \SBChord{G}
  \begin{SBVerse}
	\Ch{C}{I've} been over Snowdon, I've slept upon Crowdon
	
	I've camped by the Waynestones as \Ch{G}{well}
	
	I've sunbathed on Kinder, been burned to a cinder
	
	And many more things I can \Ch{C}{tell}
	
	\Ch{C}{My} rucksack has oft been me \Ch{G}{pillow}
	
	The heather has oft been me \Ch{C}{bed}
	
	And sooner than part from the \Ch{G}{mountains}
	
	I think I would rather be \Ch{C}{dead} \Ch{stop}{}
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBChorus}
	\Ch{C}{I'm} a rambler, I'm a rambler from \Ch{G}{Manchester} way
	
	I get all me pleasure the \Ch{C}{hard} moorland way
	
	I may be a wage slave on\Ch{G}{Monday}
	
	But I am a free man on \Ch{C}{Sunday} \Ch{stop}{}
  \end{SBChorus}
  \begin{SBVerse}
	The \Ch{C}{day} was just ending and I was descending
	
	Down Grinesbrook just by Upper \Ch{G}{Tor}
	
	When a voice cried ``Hey you'' in the way keepers do
	
	He'd the worst face that ever I \Ch{C}{saw}
	
	The things that he said were \Ch{G}{unpleasant}
	
	In the teeth of his fury I \Ch{C}{said}
	
	``Sooner than part from the \Ch{G}{mountains}
	
	I think I would rather be \Ch{C}{dead}''
  \end{SBVerse}
  \CBPageBrk
  \begin{SBVerse}
	He \Ch{C}{called} me a louse and said ``Think of the grouse''
	
	Well I thought, but I still couldn't \Ch{G}{see}
	
	Why all Kinder Scout and the moors roundabout
	
	Couldn't take both the poor grouse and \Ch{C}{me}
	
	He said ``All this land is my \Ch{G}{master's}''
	
	At that I stood shaking my \Ch{C}{head}
	
	No man has the right to own \Ch{G}{mountains}
	
	Any more than the deep ocean \Ch{C}{bed}
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBVerse}
	I \Ch{C}{once} loved a maid, a spot welder by trade
	
	She was fair as the Rowan in bloom
	
	And the bloom of her eye watched the blue Moreland sky
	
	I wooed her from April to \Ch{C}{June}
	
	On the day that we should have been \Ch{G}{married}
	
	I went for a ramble \Ch{C}{instead}
	
	For sooner than part from the \Ch{G}{mountains}
	
	I think I would rather be \Ch{C}{dead} \Ch{stop}{}
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBVerse}
	So I'll \Ch{C}{walk} where I will over mountain and hill
	
	And I'll lie where the bracken is \Ch{G}{deep}
	
	I belong to the mountains, the clear running fountains
	
	Where the grey rocks lie ragged and \Ch{C}{steep}
	
	I've seen the white hare in the \Ch{G}{gullys}
	
	And the curlew fly high over\Ch{C}{head}
	
	And sooner than part from the \Ch{G}{mountains} \Ch{stop}{}
	
	I think I would rather be \Ch{C}{dead}
  \end{SBVerse}
