\section{Getting Ready to Play}

\subsection{Tuning}

\begin{minipage}[b]{0.5\linewidth}
Attach the tuner to the headstock and turn it on.
If your tuner has different modes choose `chromatic' 
(the normal musical scale with twelve equally spaced pitches).
Pluck the string and the tuner will tell you what the note is.
Then adjust the tuning keys until the notes are:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cccc}
G & C & E & A \\
Good & Cooks & Eat & Apples \\ 
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The strings will stretch initially so that the ukulele will
go out tune within minutes of begining to play.
Just keep retuning it, after a while it will settle down and 
stay in tune.
\end{minipage}
\hspace{0.5cm}
\begin{minipage}[b]{0.5\linewidth}
\includegraphics[width=.52\textwidth,bb=0 0 248 250]{Tuning}
\end{minipage}

\subsection{Holding and Strumming}

Grab it where the neck meets the body with the strings towards you.
Bring the ukulele towards you and press it horizontally 
across your front with your arm horizontal.
You should be able to press the ukulele against yourself
with your forearm.

Strum where the neck meets the body, not over the sound hole.
