\begin{SBSong}{Ghost Chickens in the Sky}
  {A favorite with boy Scouts}
  {Sean Morey}
  \SBChord{Am}  
  \SBChord{C}
  \SBChord{F}
  \begin{SBVerse}
	\Ch{Am}{A} chicken farmer went out, one \Ch{C}{dark} and windy day,

	\Ch{Am}{By} the coop he rested as he went \Ch{C}{along} his way,

	\Ch{Am}{When} all at once a rotten egg hit him in the eye,

	\Ch{F}{It} was the sight he dreaded; Ghost \Ch{Am}{Chickens} in the Sky!
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBChorus}
	\Ch{Am}{Bawk}, Bawk, Bawk, \Ch{C}{BAWWWWK!}

	\Ch{C}{Bawk}, Bawk, Bawk, \Ch{Am}{BAWWWWK!}

	\Ch{F}{Ghost} Chickens in the \Ch{Am}{Sky!}
  \end{SBChorus}
  \begin{SBVerse}
    The \Ch{Am}{farmer}  had raised chickens since \Ch{C}{he} was twenty-four,

	\Ch{Am}{Working} for the colonel for thirty \Ch{C}{years} or more.

	\Ch{Am}{Killing} all those chickens, and sending them to fry.

	\Ch{F}{Now} they want revenge\ldots Ghost \Ch{Am}{Chickens} in the Sky!
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBVerse}
	\Ch{Am}{Their} beaks were black and shiny, their \Ch{C}{eyes} were burning red.

	\Ch{Am}{They} had no meat or feathers; these chickens \Ch{C}{were} dead!

	\Ch{Am}{They} picked the farmer up, and he died by the claw.

	\Ch{F}{They} cooked him `Extra Crispy', and they \Ch{Am}{ate} him with coleslaw!
  \end{SBVerse}
  \SBDirection{Repeat chorus several times - make the "Bawks" louder each time}
\end{SBSong}
