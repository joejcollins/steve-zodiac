\begin{SBSong}{Ghost Riders in the Sky}
  {$Id: 13GhostRidersInTheSky.tex 624 2011-07-10 12:20:41Z LudwigVanBoll@gmail.com $}
  {The chorus is sung at Aston Villa as `Holte Enders in the Sky'}
  {Stan Jones}
  \SBChord{Am}  
  \SBChord{C}
  \SBChord{F}
  \begin{SBVerse}
	An \Ch{Am}{old} cowpoke went riding out one \Ch{C}{hot} and windy day,
	
	U\Ch{Am}{pon} a ridge he rested as he \Ch{C}{went} along his way,

	When \Ch{Am}{all} at once a mighty herd of red-eyed cows he saw,

	A-\Ch{F}{plowin'} through the ragged skies, and \Ch{Am}{up} the cloudy draw.
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBChorus}
	\Ch{Am}{Yip}-i-ya-\Ch{C}{o}, 

    \Ch{C}{Yip}-i-ya-\Ch{Am}{a,} 

	\Ch{F}{Ghost} riders in the \Ch{Am}{sky}.
  \end{SBChorus}
  \begin{SBVerse}
	Their \Ch{Am}{brands} were still on fire and their \Ch{C}{hoofs} were made of steel.

	Their \Ch{Am}{horns} were black and shiny and their \Ch{C}{hot} breath he could feel.

	A \Ch{Am}{bolt} of fear went through him as they thundered through the sky.

	For \Ch{F}{he} saw the riders comin' hard, and he \Ch{Am}{heard} their mournful cry.
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBVerse}
	Their \Ch{Am}{faces} gaunt, their eyes were blurred, their \Ch{C}{shirts} all soaked with sweat,
	
	He's \Ch{Am}{riding} hard to catch that herd, but \Ch{C}{they} ain't caught him yet.
	
	They've \Ch{Am}{got} to ride forevermore on the range up in the sky,
	
	On \Ch{F}{horses} snorting fire and as they \Ch{Am}{ride}, I hear them cry.
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBVerse}
    And \Ch{Am}{as} the riders loped on by he \Ch{C}{heard} one call his name,

    If \Ch{Am}{you} want to save your soul from hell a-\Ch{C}{ridin'} on our range,

    Then \Ch{Am}{cowboy} better change your ways or with us you will ride,

    Try\Ch{F}{ing} to catch the devil's herd a\Ch{Am}{cross} these endless skies.
  \end{SBVerse}
  \SBDirection{Chorus\\Chorus}
\end{SBSong}
