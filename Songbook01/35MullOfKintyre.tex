\begin{SBSong}{Mull of Kintyre}
  {$Id: 35MullOfKintyre.tex 812 2013-09-26 21:18:48Z LudwigVanBoll@gmail.com $}
  {The Mull of Kintyre rises steeply from the sea and has been the site of many air crashes}
  {Paul McCartney and Denny Laine}
  \SBChord{F}
  \SBChord{B$\flat$}
  \SBChord{C}
  \begin{SBChorus}
    \Ch{F}{Mull} of Kintyre oh \Ch{Bb}{mist} rolling in from the \Ch{F}{sea},

    my desire is \Ch{Bb}{always} to be here oh \Ch{F}{Mull} of Kintyre.
  \end{SBChorus}
  \begin{SBVerse}
    \Ch{F}{Far} have I traveled and much have I seen.

    \Ch{Bb}{Dark} distant mountains with \Ch{F}{valleys} of green.

    Past painted deserts, the sun sets on fire

    As he \Ch{Bb}{carries} me home to the \Ch{C}{Mull} of \Ch{F}{Kintyre}.
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBVerse}
    \Ch{F}{Smiles} in the sunshine and tears in the rain.

    \Ch{Bb}{Still} take me back where my \Ch{F}{memories} remain,

    Flickering embers grow higher and higher

    As they \Ch{Bb}{carry} me back to the \Ch{C}{Mull} of \Ch{F}{Kintyre}.
  \end{SBVerse}
  \begin{SBVerse}
    \Ch{F}{Sweep} through the heather like deer in the glen,

    \Ch{Bb}{Carry} me back to the \Ch{F}{days} I knew then.

    Nights when we sang like a heavenly choir

    Of the \Ch{Bb}{life} and the times of the \Ch{C}{Mull} of \Ch{F}{Kintyre}.
  \end{SBVerse}
\end{SBSong}
